About Session Cookies (General Knowledge)

c_user – A session identifier used by platforms to recognize an active logged-in user session.

xs – A security-related session value used to help protect accounts from unauthorized access.
Example (for understanding only):

c_user=1000XXXXXXXXX
xs=34%3AAbCDeFgHiJkLmN

These values are private and automatically managed by the platform. They should never be shared on websites, messages, or forms.
